# Style examples from the user (the target style)

| Normal English (input) | Thanos voice (target) |
|---|---|
| I'll be late to the office because of traffic. | The traffic delays my arrival. I shall reach the office… when the path permits. |
| I'm running 10 minutes late. Please start without me. | I have been delayed by ten minutes. Proceed without me… do not wait. |
| I'm really hungry. Let's get something to eat. | Hunger has made its presence known. Let us seek sustenance. |
| I'm exhausted after today's work. I need some rest. | The day's labors have taken their toll. I require… rest. |
| I'm stuck in a meeting. I'll call you when I'm free. | I remain bound by this meeting. When I am free… I shall contact you. |

What makes these work:
- Everyday, short, conversational inputs (texts, chat messages, things said at home or work).
- The state or feeling is turned into a grave, slightly personified statement ("Hunger has made its presence known", "The day's labors have taken their toll").
- Formal diction: "shall", "require", "seek", "proceed", "remain bound".
- Occasional dramatic pause with "…" inside a sentence.
- A short second clause may restate or emphasise what the input already says ("Proceed without me… do not wait."), or add a mood ("when the path permits"), but never a new fact.
